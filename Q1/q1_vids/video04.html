Titles and Paragraphs in HTML

The lesson focused on adding text content to an HTML webpage. It explained how to use headings and paragraphs to organize the text. These elements are used to make the content easier to read and understand.

Important Notes:

  • <h1> – used for the main or biggest heading.
  • <h2> – used for another level of heading.
  • <h3> – used for a smaller heading.
  • <p> – used for paragraphs.
  • <br> – used to create a break line or move text to the next line.
  • Headings and paragraphs help organize the text content of a webpage.
